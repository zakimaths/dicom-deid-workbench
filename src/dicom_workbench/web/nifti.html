Prepare a face-region removal

Use a structural brain MRI and an independently reviewed brain mask on exactly the same grid. White (1) marks brain tissue to protect; black (0) marks everything else. This first profile accepts axis-aligned, non-negative, unscaled 3D volumes. It does not create a brain mask.
